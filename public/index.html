SchoolMS is a comprehensive school management platform built to digitize and streamline administrative processes. Our system handles everything from teacher management to financial reporting, making school operations efficient and transparent.

This project was developed as part of a school project to demonstrate the application of web technologies in solving real-world educational management challenges.
